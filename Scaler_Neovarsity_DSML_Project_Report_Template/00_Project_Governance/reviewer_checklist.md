# Reviewer Checklist (DSML 5-Case Portfolio, 100 Pages)

Use this checklist before final submission.

## A. Structural Completeness

- [ ] Total report length is exactly 100 pages.
- [ ] All five case studies are included.
- [ ] Each case has clear problem statement, method, findings, recommendations.
- [ ] Cross-case summary and conclusion are included.
- [ ] Appendices (references, assumptions, reproducibility) are included.

## B. Statistical and Analytical Quality

- [ ] Hypotheses are explicitly stated where relevant.
- [ ] Statistical tests are justified and interpreted correctly.
- [ ] Confidence intervals are reported with assumptions.
- [ ] Outlier and missing-value treatment is documented.
- [ ] Visualizations have titles, labels, and business interpretation.

## C. Business Relevance

- [ ] Insights are connected to business goals.
- [ ] Recommendations are specific and actionable.
- [ ] Risks, assumptions, and constraints are disclosed.
- [ ] Expected impact is quantified where possible.

## D. Reproducibility

- [ ] Dataset source and version are documented.
- [ ] Notebook/script references are provided.
- [ ] Library/tool versions are listed.
- [ ] Any manual data steps are described.

## E. Communication Quality

- [ ] Executive summary is understandable for non-technical audience.
- [ ] Terminology is consistent across chapters.
- [ ] Page numbering and section headings are consistent.
- [ ] Grammar and formatting are clean and professional.

## F. Final Submission Readiness

- [ ] All placeholders are replaced with final content.
- [ ] All figure/table references resolve correctly.
- [ ] Confidential information is removed or anonymized.
- [ ] PDF export preserves pagination and formatting.
