## Business Problem Captured

- Walmart wants to evaluate whether transaction spending differs across:
  - Gender
  - Marital status
  - Age group
- Outcomes should support targeted marketing and inventory strategy.

## Analytical Flow Observed

1. Data loading and structural checks
2. Data typing and distribution analysis
3. Missing value and outlier checks
4. Group-level spending comparisons
5. Confidence interval estimation
6. CLT demonstrations with multiple sample sizes
7. Recommendations by segment

## Key Signals Captured for Template

- Dataset shape used: 550,068 x 10
- Purchase distribution: right-skewed
- Reported average spending difference by gender
- Marital-status effect: relatively weak
- Age cohorts (especially 26 to 50) highlighted as valuable segment

## Recommended Reporting Blocks for Final Chapter
